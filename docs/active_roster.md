## Design rules

- No ordinary species has a mandatory prerequisite species or exact lure-object recipe.
- Props, weather, location, time, and resident effects contribute semantic tags.
- Species score those tags through positive and negative preferences.
- Rarity changes encounter frequency, not whether a setup is logically suitable.
- Resident perks are bonuses and alternate discovery routes, never progression gates.
- Journal knowledge should reveal qualitative observations rather than numeric weights.
- Every Camp nickname must retain an explicit original_cryptid identity.
- Art sheets are design references, not final assets. Avoid applying one uniform AI-style filter to the whole roster.

## First playable collection

Implementation should prioritize Mothling, Froglet, Sprigfoot, Stilts, Squonk, Dovi, Chup Chup, and Hoddy. Rumble is the first large special encounter and should become dramatically more plausible during storm/high/open conditions.

## Moments

Moments are small sanctuary events unlocked by residents and resident pairs. Initial examples:

- Mothling: watches a lantern.
- Sprigfoot: leaves absurdly large tracks for such a tiny resident.
- Stilts: crosses camp under moonlight.
- Froglet + Squonk: Froglet discovers Squonk's new puddle.
- Dovi + Mothling: silently stare at the same light.
- Chup Chup: a shiny decoration goes missing and is found behind a rock.
- Chup Chup + Snally: both compete for the same shiny object.
- Rumble + Stilts: Stilts casually walks beneath the enormous Thunderbird.
- Mapi + Froglet: Froglet rides on Mapi through the water.

Moments should primarily add personality, journal observations, and small world reactions rather than mandatory rewards.

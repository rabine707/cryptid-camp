# Cryptid Camp — Game Design v0.1

## Fantasy
Build temporary **Lure Sites** to attract mysterious creatures. Observe evidence, identify them, meet persistent individuals, earn their trust, and invite them to a permanent **Sanctuary**.

## Core loop
**Rumor → Build Lure Site → Observe → Trail Cam → Evidence → Discovery → Trust → Adoption → Sanctuary → Bond**

## Principles
1. Cryptids are discovered through curiosity, with field notes that prevent blind setup guessing.
2. The player never sees raw attraction percentages.
3. Longer waits produce more events, not pay-to-skip advantages.
4. An adopted cryptid is permanent. No storage-pressure releasing.
5. Individuals matter: species, variant, size, personality, quirk, trust, history, and name.
6. Rare variants come primarily from discoverable conditions rather than loot-box odds.
7. No combat is required for the core fantasy.

## Prototype world
Whispering Woods is a small temporary lure clearing. Initial species are Mothling, Bigfoot, and Fresno Nightcrawler. The first implementation focuses only on Mothling.

## Trust
Wary → Curious → Comfortable → Friendly → Bonded → Family. At Bonded, a cryptid may choose to follow the player home. Three varied actions per meeting provide enough trust for even a cautious Mothling to reach Bonded within three meetings; trying a different action earns a small connection bonus.

## Setup guidance
Every Field Kit object shows the attraction traits it contributes. The lure-site header reads the current setup and names its strongest visitor match without revealing roll percentages. Data-authored Field Notes provide one practical recipe per available visitor, including required objects and things to avoid.

## Sanctuary
Adopted cryptids roam, can be named and cared for, and interact with decorations. First Moment: **Warm Glow** — Mothling hugs a lamp.
